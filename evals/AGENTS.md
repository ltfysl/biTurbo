## evals

Evaluation fixtures and golden datasets for recall, search, and ingest quality.

## Rules

- Keep data deterministic and versioned.
- Do not include PII or proprietary code snippets without permission.
- Update golden sets when behavior intentionally changes.
- Small JSON/TS fixtures only; do not store large models here.

## Best practices

- Name fixtures by the test or scenario they support.
- Document expected results or scores in the fixture or a sibling markdown file.
