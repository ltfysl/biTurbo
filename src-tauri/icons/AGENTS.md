## src-tauri/icons

Application icon set in the formats required by Tauri bundles.

## Rules

- Keep source PNGs high-resolution and optimized.
- Generate `.ico` and `.icns` from the source PNGs when branding changes.
- Do not commit multi-megabyte images unnecessarily.

## Best practices

- Maintain the `icon.png`, `32x32.png`, `128x128.png`, and `128x128@2x.png` set.
- Update all formats together to keep branding consistent across platforms.
